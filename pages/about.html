About Us

ElFahham Company was established in 1972, where we, the sons of the late Saad Abu Bakr Al-
Fahham, began trading in agricultural crops, limiting ourselves to local work only at the beginning.
After seven years, we worked hard to develop production capacity and purchase the latest
equipment and machines to obtain high-quality results in the fastest time. In 1990, we expanded our
areas of work and introduced more products to the Egyptian market.

The Turning Point in the History of
ElFahham

At the beginning of the 2000s, we decided to expand our activity
and increase our efforts to meet the needs of the Egyptian
market.

We opened a new headquarters for sorting, packing and
exporting rice. We were also able to provide luxurious, high-
quality rice to the Egyptian market.

In 2012, the company became a famous brand under the name
“ElFahham for Processing, Importing and Exporting Agricultural
Crops.”

ElFahham: A Success Story

Mr. Hesham Mohamed Elfahham had a pivotal role in building and developing the company’s business on his
own, away from the family business. The company enhanced its production capacity in an unprecedented
way. The company now has more than 7 production lines, with a daily production of 500 tons.

We also created storage spaces with a capacity of 20,000 tons, in addition to cold storage areas equipped
with the highest quality.

Our Path to Globalism

ElFahham Company has become among the largest import and export companies for various goods.

We have exported more than 30 thousand tons of white beans, Egyptian beans, sesame, lentils and
spices to many countries, and imported many products needed by the Egyptian market such as
condiment such as black pepper and nuts.
